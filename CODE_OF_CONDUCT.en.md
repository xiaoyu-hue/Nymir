# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using friendly and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- Issues, Pull Requests, and discussions in the project repository
- Public communication within the project community
- An individual representing the project or community in public spaces
- Any individual using an official project identity

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported via project Issues or email.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will evaluate and determine all consequences based on this Code of Conduct, and will take whatever action they deem appropriate.
